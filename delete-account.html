How to Delete Your Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Typing Football League account and what happens to your data when you do.

Steps to delete your account

  1. Open Typing Football League and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap Options, then Delete Account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete My Account.

Deletion happens immediately — there is no waiting period, and it cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your player profile, including your display name, coin balance, XP, and level
  • Your full game history and stats
  • Any feedback or bug reports you've submitted

All of this data is permanently and immediately erased from our servers. We do not retain it for any period after deletion, and it cannot be recovered.

Active games at the time of deletion

If you delete your account while a multiplayer game is still in progress, that game is closed out immediately. Your opponent's wager for that game is refunded to them, and they're notified that the game ended because you deleted your account.
